Harmonic

360-degree employee evaluations as a multi-tenant SaaS. An LLM turns the review answers into an anonymised feedback report. My favourite part was the anonymity math: if one group's scores are hidden, all of them are, otherwise you could subtract the shown groups from the overall score and find the small one. Freelance, MVP delivered in September 2026.

NestJS, TypeScript, Kafka, PostgreSQL, TypeORM, JWT, Claude / OpenAI / Gemini, Docker, GitLab CI
